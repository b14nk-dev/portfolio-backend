## JobRadar

Rust, Axum, Tokio, SQLite, HTMX and a small Node browser helper. A local job
discovery and tracking dashboard that collects public listings, ranks them
against a JavaScript/TypeScript profile and tracks pending, applied, skipped and
banned jobs. It does not submit applications automatically.

## JustMyFare

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL and Sequelize. A
personal flight-booking platform split into a customer site, CRM and backend,
with flight search, passenger and booking workflows, permissions, migrations
and repeatable database bootstrap tooling.

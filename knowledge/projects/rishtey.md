# Project — Rishtey (Matchmaking Platform)

- **Type:** Professional / office — July 2023 – November 2025
- **Region:** NCR (Delhi National Capital Region)
- **Live:** <https://rishteyncr.com/>
- **Stack:** Next.js, React, Node.js, Express, MySQL, Sequelize, Redux Toolkit,
  Jotai, Tailwind CSS, NextAuth, JWT, AWS S3, PayU, Sharp

A subscription matchmaking platform with profiles, interests, shortlists,
messaging, plans and onboarding for **~8,000 user profiles**.

- **Profiles** — personal, family, lifestyle and preference details; photo
  galleries; completion and visibility controls.
- **Matchmaking** — search, interests sent/received, shortlists, saved
  profiles, views and recommendations.
- **Subscriptions** — plans, pricing, payment initiation and callbacks,
  upgrades and premium access.
- **Engagement and ops** — messaging, notifications, success stories, blogs,
  reports, account deletion, admin dashboard, content and OTP flows.

Highlights: Next.js frontend with API routes plus an Express layer on
MySQL/Sequelize; PayU subscriptions with callbacks and entitlement checks; JWT
and NextAuth sessions with OTP and password reset; S3 uploads with server-side
image processing; **35%+** faster page loads via SSR, query optimization and
lazy loading.

The product combines a public discovery and onboarding experience with an
authenticated member area and administration workflows. The engineering work
therefore spans search and profile visibility, media handling, subscription
entitlements, account recovery and moderation-oriented operations rather than
only static profile pages.
